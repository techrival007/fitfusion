**PROBLEM STATEMENT**

The goal of FITFUSION is to create technological solutions addressing the following fitness and wellness challenges faced within a campus environment:

### **1\. Nutrition Awareness within Daily Campus Life**

* Students’ nutrition habits are closely tied to their daily routines, mental state, and activity levels, yet food intake is often tracked in isolation without context.  
* Solution: Design a system that helps users log campus food intake and understand calorie and nutrient consumption in relation to their daily activities and well-being, enabling more informed nutrition decisions over time.

### **2\. Mental Well-being as a Component of Fitness**

* Mental well-being strongly influences consistency in physical activity, eating habits, and overall lifestyle, but is rarely considered alongside fitness data.  
* Solution: Enable users to reflect on their mental state through mood logs and journals.  
  


  ### **3\. Campus Fitness Patterns & Collective Insights**

* Individual activity data provides limited value without broader context, while collective patterns can motivate engagement and awareness when viewed responsibly.  
* Solution: Analyze activity, nutrition, and participation data to generate anonymized summaries and comparative views across hostels, academic years, or departments, highlighting how fitness behavior evolves across the campus community.

### **4\. Environmental Conditions Affecting Fitness and Well-being**

* Environmental factors such as air quality, noise levels, crowding, and weather directly impact outdoor activity, exercise quality, and mental comfort, yet are often disconnected from fitness planning.  
* **Solution:** Incorporate environmental quality indicators into the system to help users and administrators understand how surroundings influence physical activity, well-being trends, and campus fitness participation.

**EVALUATION CRITERIA**

### **(a). Application Development (Weightage: 50%)**

Develop an application, preferably a mobile or web application, with appropriate user interfaces such as:

* Admin interface  
* Student/Faculty interface  
* Analytics or dashboard interface

### **(b). Security and Robustness (Weightage: 25%)**

Ensure proper security and privacy measures for user data and access control.

### **(c). UI/UX Design (Weightage: 15%)**

A clear and intuitive UI/UX design of the proposed application, preferably demonstrated using design tools such as Figma.

### **(d). Technical Design**
